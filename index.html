Sensor phenotyping detects treatment response

AI-guided drug discovery and sensor-based phenotyping were used to test resveratrol in human cortical organoids exposed to blast injury. The supplied research example shows a partial shift toward the uninjured state, supported by follow-up biochemical evidence of restored neurofilament levels and reduced phosphorylated tau.

Read a complex biological state

Inflammation, metabolic stress and cellular damage can contribute to a complex biological state. NanoGlyph captures a combined molecular fingerprint to help compare phenotypes when a single marker does not tell the whole story.

An ALS-associated fingerprint in serum

Our research combines nanosensor measurements and deep learning to distinguish ALS from control serum. Disease-associated spectral fingerprints were supported by proteomic analysis of sensor-bound proteins.

Follow biological change over time

Read the same culture at multiple timepoints and follow how its optical fingerprint evolves. Repeated, time-resolved measurements depend on an assay that preserves sample integrity.

Prioritize samples for deeper study

Screen many conditions, compare molecular fingerprints and select informative samples for focused follow-up with omics, targeted assays or imaging.

From sensor corona to biomarker candidates

Pair optical fingerprints with recovery of sensor-bound proteins and LC-MS/MS proteomics to prioritize candidate biomarkers. This downstream workflow identifies candidates for independent validation; the optical fingerprint alone does not identify a specific protein.
